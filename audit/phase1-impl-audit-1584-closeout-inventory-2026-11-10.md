## Controlling accepted Phase 1 assurance claim

The implemented **bounded** native claim is: `closeSourceManifestBindingForInstance` checks a *selected accepted* `GenericApplicationAssurance` against a complete supplied `ArchitectureInstanceDescriptor` and its canonical identity in a checked `VerificationBundle`, then `closeVerificationBundle` closes a manifest; `bindProducedFinalAuthority` compares the resulting **whole** manifest and source assurance against separately accepted objects; `bindNativeFinalAuthority` requires the **whole** `StageClosureBundle` to equal the one in the independently accepted `CertifiedReleaseArtifact`, checks raw Systems digest and lowering root, and projects exact typed stage coordinates. `certifyReleaseArtifact` independently verifies the actual manifest, stage, emitted LLVM and declared residual trust. These functions do not by themselves establish that the checked architecture occurrence produced the selected concrete stage or that their accepted objects are faithfully reflected into the Rocq semantic final-authority theorem.

Native entry points: `src/Phil/Verification/{SourceManifestBinding,ManifestClosure,FinalAuthorityAdapter,CertifiedRelease,NativeFinalInstanceCorrespondence}.hs`, `Phil.Verification.ArtifactClosure`, `Phil.Systems.StageClosure`, and `Phil.Assurance.Verify`. Conditional model/proof paths: `proof/Phil/Verification/{NativeFinalInstanceTypedCorrespondence,FinalManifestFixedAdapter,FinalManifestFixedAdapterCertification,FinalManifestNativeCoordinateReflection,FinalManifestStageReflection}.v`. Exact compiled assumption queries are enumerated in `.github/workflows/phase1-audit-native-final-instance-typed-correspondence.yml`; CI success is **not** itself a native-to-Rocq representation theorem.

- Unlisted declaration or invalid verification inputs fail at `closeSourceManifestBinding` / `closeVerificationBundle`; substituted whole produced manifest or source assurance fails at `bindProducedFinalAuthority`.
