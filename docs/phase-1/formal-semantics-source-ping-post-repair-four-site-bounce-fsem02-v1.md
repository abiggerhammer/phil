**Status: bounded, non-normative; FSEM-02 remains open.** This extends the [triple-site reinjection check](formal-semantics-source-ping-post-repair-triple-reinjection-fsem02-v1.md) from monotone repairs to one non-monotone regression, not to a new source permission.

## Typing, state, transitions, and witnesses

The accepted [Grammar-v1 typed Ping source](../../test/fixtures/fsem02-protocol-progression-v1/accepted.phil) uses U8 Client Send / Server Receive and End Done. Its minimal [stale Close counterexample](../../test/fixtures/fsem02-protocol-progression-v1/stale-close.phil) must still reject through the production Surface checker as \`GrammarV1ComponentSendCloseCloseMismatch\`, not a parse error or generic opaque success.

The [production Haskell test](../../test/Phase1FSEM02ProtocolProgressionMain.hs) starts with 576 source-derived, independently hostile Ping/Close contexts: 9 partner placements × 4 already committed Close prefixes × 4 partner suffix orders × 4 endpoint-resource shape cases. After explicitly restoring the two live peers' Client/Server session heads and Client/Server linear predecessor resources, the test **reintroduces all four faults**, then tries each of **24 repair orders**. The first repaired observation is deliberately recontaminated once before being repaired again. All 13,824 non-monotone repair traces must reject at every incomplete prefix with the exact earliest outstanding Client-head, Server-head, Client-resource or Server-resource error, without changing committed terminal Close state, independent affine spectator loan, or other owners. Only the fully recovered state may replay the exact original, externally admitted Ping/Close suffix.

Recontamination and restoration are **external test interventions, not Phil source transitions**. Source syntax supplies no nominal protocol instance, role, activation, partner, scheduler, disposal, selected-root, terminal or assurance authority; these are explicit external premises. The [Rocq 9.2 model](../../proof/Phil/Core/FSEM02PingPostRepairFourSiteBounce.v) proves that a first-site repair followed by recontamination restores the all-fault mask, and conditionally classifies the ensuing first native-style refusal versus the separately admitted full-repair transition. Neither this finite model nor source/native tests constitute universal Haskell↔Rocq refinement, fairness, liveness, or full Phase 1 semantics.

The [unprivileged focused workflow](../../.github/workflows/fsem02-post-repair-four-site-bounce-preflight.yml) requires warning-free strict production Haskell/source regression and admission-free Rocq verification on the exact work HEAD before a draft PR. All subsequent required exact-HEAD checks, fresh post-ready FSEM claim guard, normal protected expected-HEAD merge, and verified release remain mandatory.
