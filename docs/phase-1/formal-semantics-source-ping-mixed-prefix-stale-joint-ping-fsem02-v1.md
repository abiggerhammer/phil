# FSEM-02: stale joint Ping refusal at every mixed terminal prefix

**Status:** bounded, non-normative source/Core regression with separately conditional Rocq phase laws. Tracks [#1613](https://github.com/abiggerhammer/phil/issues/1613) and follows [mixed-prefix Close classification](formal-semantics-source-ping-mixed-prefix-fault-retry-fsem02-v1.md).

The accepted [Grammar-v1 Ping source](../../test/fixtures/fsem02-protocol-progression-v1/accepted.phil) is checked for exact Send/Receive endpoint identities and typed End continuations. The minimally rejected [stale-Close source](../../test/fixtures/fsem02-protocol-progression-v1/stale-close.phil) must still reject with `GrammarV1ComponentSendCloseCloseMismatch`, identifying `done` versus `endpoint`, not a generic syntax error.

The production [three-pair Haskell test](../../test/Phase1FSEM02ProtocolProgressionMain.hs) walks all **13,440 legal mixed orders** of three independently activated joint Ping rendezvous and six local terminal Closes, visiting ten prefixes per schedule. At each prefix it inspects three *original* joint requests. A not-yet-started pair must retain both exact source-typed linear predecessors; a pair that has already rendezvoused must have **neither** predecessor owner, and replay through the actual `checkProcessCommunication` must reject with the precise `RendezvousEndpointNotOwnedByProcess` constructor, naming its original Client process and Client predecessor. Each successful Ping is atomic with respect to its paired predecessor consumption, even if unrelated pairs have already Closed. This produces **302,400 deliberately refused stale joint Ping attempts**. The previous **806,400 rejected Close probes** remain active; every legal suffix still reaches the canonical six-process terminal state, preserving the independently borrowed affine spectator.

The separately compiled [six Rocq 9.2 lemmas](../../proof/Phil/Core/FSEM02PingMixedPrefixStalePing.v) model conditional pair consumption, refusal once either participant is marked consumed, and unaffected unrelated participants. The `nat` identifiers and Boolean consumption ledger are **abstract surrogates**, not an extracted representation of Haskell `ProcessKey` or the native process/resource maps. The lemmas do not establish error-constructor fidelity or a native implementation-refinement theorem.

Protocol instance, role authority, original activation, selected root, scheduler, disposal permission and assurance remain **external premises**. The tests do not grant new nominal authority, normative semantics, general deadlock freedom or FSEM-02 completion. Related [SessionStep.v](../../proof/Phil/Core/SessionStep.v) and [ConcurrencySemantics.v](../../proof/Phil/Core/ConcurrencySemantics.v) remain separate, non-identical models.
