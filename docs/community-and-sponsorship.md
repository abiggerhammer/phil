# Community & Sponsorship

Phil is a systems programming language in which **architecture is executable and implementation is replaceable**.

It is developed in the open as part of the Logics to Order research program, with contributions and support from researchers, engineers, independent builders, and an enthusiastic community.

## Get involved

There are many ways to contribute:

- **Code and examples:** Implement language features, exercise existing functionality, and contribute example programs.
- **Formal verification:** Review specifications, proofs, proof assumptions, and correspondence claims.
- **Testing and auditing:** Find counterexamples, investigate unsupported cases, and report what Phil cannot yet establish.
- **Documentation:** Help make the language accessible to new users and formal-methods researchers.
- **Infrastructure and sponsorship:** Support the computing resources and engineering work needed to develop and verify Phil.

You can get started through the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/abiggerhammer/phil) and its [issue tracker](https://github.com/abiggerhammer/phil/issues).

Finding a limitation is a contribution. A useful report explains what was checked, what remains unproved, and how the missing guarantee matters to a real application.

We appreciate the people and organizations helping make Phil's development possible.

[Higher.fun](https://higher.fun/) has provided financial support for Phil's development and helped introduce the language to a community of independent developers.

Sponsorship supports development; it does not determine Phil's language semantics, proof obligations, acceptance criteria, or technical roadmap.

Maintainers may receive sponsorship or tokens personally. Personal holdings are distinct from project funds and do not automatically constitute a Phil project treasury.

This information is provided for identification and transparency, not as an invitation or recommendation to purchase or trade cryptocurrency. Tokens can be highly volatile.

## Independent development

Phil is intended to be useful regardless of who implements it, which backend it targets, or whether its users participate in any token community.

Independent implementations, applications, experiments, and critical reviews are welcome.

Contributions are evaluated on their technical merits. No sponsorship relationship or token ownership changes the standards for accepting code or establishing proof claims.

**Architecture is executable. Implementation is replaceable. The community is open.**
